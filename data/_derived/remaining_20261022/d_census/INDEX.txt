DEFINITIONS USED
  bucket            (company, quarter) master block, PL_breakdown items 1/16-32 (Tier-1) and CSM_waterfall items 1-6.  Vintage evidence = DART FS-API original column (thstrm) versus the next-year filing's prior column (frmtrm);
                    master vs prior when the original has no API (2023.1Q/2Q).  Both columns are OFS (separate) in every bucket, no unit ratio anywhere.
  sign artifact     |later| == |original| with opposite sign, or items 18/19 derived from such lines while items 1/17/20/22/24 are equal.
  closing           (a) Tier-1 identities on the later vector: 20=1+17, |22-24|=|23|, 31=24+25 (gate tolerances); (b) the LOB bridge item1 = legs + 15 - 16 still closes with the current Tier-2 legs
                    (item 1/15/16 equal, or offsetting); (c) CSM: later prior column equals the master (scan SAME), or it differs but the PL4<->CSM5 pair still passes AND the same-FY CSM chain is adoptable as a unit
                    AND no owner gold conflicts AND the reader reproduced the master on the same filing; (d) gate simulation without new RED/FAIL.  Tier-2 prior-column readers do not exist, so (b) is a proxy.
  screen change     material = any displayed item moves >= 20 eok, or a headline (보험손익/투자손익/당기순이익) >= 5%.  Displayed: compare.html 보험손익/투자손익/당기순이익/ROE/기말 CSM, IFRS17.html P3 bridge + OCI zone,
                    CSM waterfall, csm_combo, index.html bubble, NB_CSM_multiple.  Items 18-20, 22, 25 are download-only (xlsx 손익분해PL / public_exports).
  seam              derived 값_당분기 straddling two vintages; Kyobo precedent = blank (null_dangi), owner rule 2026-10-22.
  gold              cell level (an item the adoption would change is an owner gold cell).  The 2026-10-12 dry run used the block level; 10 of the 19 owner buckets carry gold elsewhere in the block and were held then.
